# E-Commerce Sales — Exploratory Data Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on the **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** of an e-commerce sales dataset containing **1,200 orders**.

The objective was to explore the dataset, understand customer and sales patterns, identify trends, examine distributions, detect outliers, and generate meaningful business insights that could support further analysis and decision-making.

This project represents the **discovery phase** of the DecodeLabs Data Analytics project, following the data cleaning and preparation stage.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The analysis was designed to:

* Calculate descriptive statistics such as mean, median, count, minimum, and maximum.
* Understand the distribution of key numerical variables.
* Identify trends across time.
* Analyze sales performance across products and business dimensions.
* Explore relationships between numerical variables.
* Detect and investigate potential outliers.
* Identify patterns that could provide useful business insights.
* Translate analytical findings into clear, actionable observations.

## 🔎 Analysis Performed

### 1. Descriptive Statistics

Key numerical variables were analyzed using measures including:

* Count
* Mean
* Median
* Minimum
* Maximum
* Standard deviation

| Metric    | Quantity | Unit Price | Total Revenue | Items in Cart |
| --------- | -------: | ---------: | ------------: | ------------: |
| Count     |    1,200 |      1,200 |         1,200 |         1,200 |
| Mean      |     2.95 |     356.41 |      1,053.97 |          5.49 |
| Median    |     3.00 |     364.21 |        823.62 |          5.00 |
| Minimum   |        1 |      11.39 |         11.39 |             1 |
| Maximum   |        5 |     699.93 |      3,456.40 |            10 |
| Std. Dev. |     1.41 |     197.18 |        819.86 |          2.28 |

---

### 2. Distribution Analysis

The distribution of **Total Revenue** was found to be **right-skewed**.

The mean order value (**1,053.97**) is considerably higher than the median (**823.62**), indicating that a smaller number of high-value orders are pulling the average upward.

This makes the median a useful measure when describing the typical order value.

---

### 3. Outlier Analysis

The **Interquartile Range (IQR)** method was used to identify potential outliers.

| Variable      | Number of Outliers |
| ------------- | -----------------: |
| Quantity      |                  0 |
| Unit Price    |                  0 |
| Total Revenue |              **8** |
| Items in Cart |                  0 |

Only **Total Revenue** contained statistical outliers.

The highest recorded order value was **3,456.40**, compared with an upper IQR boundary of approximately **3,330.41**.

These observations were treated as values requiring investigation rather than automatically being classified as errors or removed from the dataset.

## 📈 Correlation Analysis

Correlation analysis was performed to examine relationships between numerical variables.

| Variables                     | Correlation |
| ----------------------------- | ----------: |
| Unit Price ↔ Total Revenue    |   **0.717** |
| Quantity ↔ Total Revenue      |   **0.615** |
| Quantity ↔ Items in Cart      |   **0.650** |
| Items in Cart ↔ Total Revenue |   **0.393** |
| Quantity ↔ Unit Price         |   **0.015** |
| Unit Price ↔ Items in Cart    |   **0.001** |

### Key Observation

**Unit Price had the strongest relationship with Total Revenue**, followed by Quantity.

Quantity and Unit Price themselves had almost no relationship, suggesting that higher-priced products were not necessarily associated with larger quantities purchased.

> Correlation indicates association, not causation.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### Customer Behavior

The dataset contains **1,189 unique customers across 1,200 orders**.

* **1,178 customers** placed exactly one order.
* Only **11 customers** placed more than one order.
* The maximum number of orders from a single customer was 2.

This indicates a very high proportion of one-time purchasers and suggests that **customer retention and repeat purchases could be an important area for further investigation**.

### Product Performance

* **Chair** generated the highest total revenue at approximately **195,620**.
* **Printer** was almost identical at approximately **195,613**.
* **Laptop** had the highest average order value at approximately **1,110.56**.
* **Phone** had the lowest average order value at approximately **972.58**.

### Referral Sources

* **Instagram** generated the highest total revenue at approximately **275,285**.
* **Facebook** recorded the highest average order value at approximately **1,098.29**.

This suggests that the source generating the most revenue does not necessarily generate the highest-value individual orders.

### Order Status

Cancelled orders recorded the highest average order value at approximately **1,105.58**, while returned orders recorded the lowest at approximately **984.93**.

Cancelled and returned orders collectively represented **497 of the 1,200 orders (41.4%)**, making this an area worth investigating further.

> This figure is presented as the proportion of orders in these statuses and is not interpreted as a failure rate.

### Payment Methods

Credit Card transactions had the highest average order value at approximately **1,127.55**.

## 🧠 Key Takeaways

The analysis highlighted several areas that would be valuable for deeper investigation:

1. **Customer retention** — the dataset has a very high proportion of one-time customers.
2. **Order value distribution** — revenue is right-skewed due to a small number of high-value orders.
3. **Product performance** — products generate relatively similar overall revenue but differ in average order value.
4. **Marketing channels** — Instagram leads in total revenue, while Facebook leads in average order value.
5. **Order outcomes** — cancelled and returned orders account for a substantial portion of the dataset and warrant further investigation.
6. **Revenue drivers** — Unit Price and Quantity have the strongest relationships with Total Revenue.
7. **Outlier investigation** — eight unusually high-value orders were identified and should be reviewed for context.
